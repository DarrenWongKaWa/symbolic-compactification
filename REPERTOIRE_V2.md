## B. Supported capabilities

- Exact, fail-closed equivalence adjudication over the documented parser
  whitelist.
- Immutable raw-expression hashing and sessioned provenance.
- Promotion only on exact ZERO with explicit current/candidate binding.
- Preservation of NONZERO residuals/counterexamples and UNKNOWN proof gaps.
- Structure-first inspection, including sums, products, Piecewise blocks,
  indexed calls, free symbols, and operation counts.
- Explicit symbol/assumption declarations and rejection of underspecified
  verifier domains.
- Source-grounded package and obligation validation for supported scalar
  expressions.
- First-order LGG/substitution-family observation as a limited structural
  baseline, not general representation invention.
- Deterministic compilation and hashing of RepresentationGrammarV1 programs
  as research infrastructure.

## C. Unsupported capabilities

- General mathematical representation invention.
- Scientifically validated R3+ program search.
- AI-guided mathematical search advantage.
- A benefit from verifier feedback on search navigation.
- A benefit from SOL-conditioned search.
- Held-out `ssc-representation-search-bench-v0.1` performance.
- General matrix/tensor/block-operator scientific IR.
- Exact certification of arbitrary limits or special-function identities.
- Automatic authorization of physical assumptions, domains, boundary
  conditions, symmetry changes, or integration by parts.

## D. Deprecated approaches

No frozen evidence is deleted. The following are deprecated as forward claims:

- free-form LLM output as the principal representation-invention mechanism;
- specialist terminology frequency as a proxy for certified depth;
- TYPE_ONLY labels as operational representations;
- global unrestricted `simplify()` as a discovery method;
- finite Laurent coefficients as proof of an exact remainder/limit claim;
- treating parser coverage, packaging, or authored reference programs as AI
  success.

The RPS V1 search stack is not deprecated as software; it is retained as
implemented-but-unevaluated research infrastructure.

## E. Frozen negative results

- Free-form LLM representation advantage is not supported by the preceding
  held-out experiment.
- SOL was not uniformly helpful and sometimes increased anchoring/tautology in
  that experiment.
- No certified R3+ free-form capability was established.
- Guo remains sealed at `G0016 -> G0013 = UNKNOWN LEVEL_B` under frozen
  assumptions.
- RPS V1 stopped before scientific treatment; it provides no support for
  structured-search invention and no algorithmic null result.
- The terminal context-grounded campaign found only one admissible task across
  seven searched families, below its three-task/three-family threshold. It ran
  no model or context comparison and provides no AI or context-effect estimate.
- `PACKAGING_GAP`, `TYPE_ONLY`, reference-program ZERO, and evaluator-only
  traps are not program-search successes.

The durable registry is `NEGATIVE_RESULTS.md`.

## F. Verification semantics

`ZERO` is exact symbolic proof and is the only promotion verdict. `NONZERO` is
an exact disproval backed by a provably nonzero exact probe and preserves the
residual/counterexample. `UNKNOWN` is a proof gap and never promotes.

Lifecycle and scientific axes remain separate: proposals are hypotheses;
ZERO is `PROVEN`; NONZERO is `REFUTED`; UNKNOWN is `PROOF_REQUIRED` unless a
genuinely new scientific assumption creates a `HUMAN_REQUIRED` gate.
Verification is mandatory for every promoted transformation.

## G. Assumption semantics

Assumptions must be `DECLARED` or `DERIVED` for every verifier-domain
predicate. Missing physical folklore is not inferred. New positivity,
nonzero, reality, boundary, symmetry, or limit-order choices require the human.

An open contract defect remains: documented `real:false` means complex-probe
selection, while the current SymPy construction can mean provably non-real.
Affected claims fail closed. This defect is not repaired inside the RPS
experiment.

## L. Recommended workflow for future users

1. Preserve the original expression bytes under `workspace/input/raw/`.
2. Declare the exact symbol namespace and assumptions.
3. Inspect the highest-level structure before lowering or expansion.
4. Write each candidate expression to a file.
5. Record every proposal and adjudicate it through an exact session.
6. Promote only on ZERO; preserve and inspect NONZERO/UNKNOWN evidence.
7. Use the finalizer to render the explicit certified form and provenance.
8. Treat packaging or unsupported scientific IR as a separate limitation, not
   as mathematical proof or AI evidence.

## M. Recommended workflow for future research agents

1. Read this repertoire, `CAPABILITIES.json`, and `NEGATIVE_RESULTS.md` before
   proposing a line.
2. Start from the closed evidence rather than reopening free-form prompt tuning,
   SOL rescue, Guo, or a parser-expansion explanation.
3. Predeclare the scientific estimand and assemble a fresh, assumption-complete,
   nonduplicate calibration suite before method engineering.
4. Keep reference programs hidden from search and separate reachability tests
   from discovery evaluation.
5. Freeze admissible DEV semantics before optimization and freeze a fresh TEST
   before final method selection.
6. Match frontier/batch/state budgets and record only auditable structured LLM
   decisions, usage, and hashes.
7. Report missing or unestimable outcomes explicitly; never synthesize zero
   denominators.

Do not immediately create Representation Search V2. A legitimate restart
requires a new protocol, new method version, new cases untouched by V1
reachability engineering, and an independently cleared pre-treatment gate.

## N. What must never be claimed

- That LLMs invented a general mathematical representation in this repository.
- That structured search, AI guidance, verifier feedback, or SOL failed in RPS
  V1; none received a scientific trial.
- That C9H4 or Q7V3 was discovered by search.
- That named Hermite primitives or primitive composition demonstrated invention.
- That packaging gaps are wins, failures of mathematics, or evidence about AI.
- That Guo was solved, disproved, or assumption-completed.
- That numeric agreement, finite expansion, or global simplification is exact
  certification.
- That an absent benchmark produces a success rate of zero.

## O. No automatic next research frontier

The terminal context-grounded campaign is closed. No new
representation-discovery, representation-search, verifier, Hermite, or
benchmark campaign is automatically authorized. A future effort would need a
new explicit human decision and its own predeclared protocol. Until then, the
production repertoire remains exact verification plus structure observation,
grounding, and assumption discipline; program search remains research code.
