\newcommand{\Lor}[1]{\frac{2\Gamma}{#1^2+\Gamma^2}}

A Lorentzian of width $\Gamma>0$ splits into a retarded and an advanced pole,
\begin{equation}
  \Lor{\omega} = \frac{\ii}{\omega + \ii\Gamma} - \frac{\ii}{\omega - \ii\Gamma} .
  \label{eq:lorentzian-split}
\end{equation}
