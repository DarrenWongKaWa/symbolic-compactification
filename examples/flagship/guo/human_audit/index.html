Status legend

These labels are distinct kinds of evidence. They are not a single ordered scale from bad to good.

EXACT_ZEROEncoded direct residual simplified to exact zero. Not a statement that the whole paper is verified.
ZERO_UNDER_SUBSTITUTIONDirect printed-form residual was not zero; after an explicit source-grounded substitution or identity, the residual was exactly zero. Not an unconditional engine ZERO.
CERTIFIED_BY_RULEA local machine-checkable identity plus an explicitly declared mathematical rule or domain supports the parent step. Parent is not engine ZERO. Child ZERO plus a rule is not parent ZERO.
UNKNOWN_REMAINDERFinite or local checks do not certify the stated asymptotic remainder. Not a claim that the expansion is false.
UNKNOWNA recorded limit or similar claim is not certified. Not merged into UNKNOWN_REMAINDER on this page.
STRUCTURALDefinition, bookkeeping, or no equality claim to verify. Not a failed check.
UNSUPPORTEDThe current verifier cannot honestly lower the claimed relation. Not a refutation of the paper.
NONZEROAn exact direct check refuted the encoded equality under the current assumptions. Final NONZERO = 0 on this paper. Direct NONZERO before a recorded substitution is part of ZERO_UNDER_SUBSTITUTION, not by itself a paper error.
