1. Summary (title, `AUDIT_INCOMPLETE`, metrics, colour bar, one-sentence warning).

3. Major claims (compact).
4. Central derivation (`from → to | operation | status`).
5. Reviewer queue (actionable items; one Accept-warning).

wrapped in `\(` `\)`.
