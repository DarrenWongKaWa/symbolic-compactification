# Reviewer 1 — sympathetic AI-for-Science

Recommendation if submitted **now**: **Reject, encourage resubmission**
to an AI-for-Science workshop after C1 agent experiments.

The fail-closed residual protocol is the right attitude for symbolic
physics. The Guo case study is refreshingly honest (certified-shallow,
no fake PRB discovery). Tier A soundness (0 false promotions, 35/35
label match) is a real engineering result.

It is not a conference method paper: no multi-model table, no B4
comparison, compactness lost to SymPy on the easy set, n=7 compactify
test items a graduate student can finish by hand. The related-work
positioning against LGuess / O-Forge / Moxia / Shih 2026 would have
to be the **introduction**, not an afterthought, and even then the
empirical delta is missing.

What would change my mind: B4 vs B7 on a **hard** compactify set with
5 seeds and two model families, plus one physics case that is not Guo
and not a doubled sum.
