# Independent admission audit — `rps-case-q7v3`

## Verdict

`REJECT` for scientific DEV admission. The package is a mechanically valid,
non-tautological R3 compiler/search diagnostic, but it is not a fresh
scientific identity under the frozen case-mining rules.

## What passed

- The independently downloaded arXiv `2203.03930v2` archive has the declared
  SHA-256. The complete TeX and stored lines 367–373 also match, and the PDF
  labels the block Theorem 2, equations (8)–(9).
- The scalar namespace and domain predicates are sufficient for every public
  rational expression.
- The public loader reads exactly the seven declared files. Target/operator
  terms, node sequences, multiplicities, derivative order, source identity,
  hidden roles, reference programs, and receipts are not public.
- Exact matrix-unit path enumeration leaves one permutation per member.
  Independent residue calculations give ZERO against all three member files.
- M1 canonically compiles `G_FULL`, `G_NO_HERMITE`, and `G_PRIMITIVE` with
  three non-tautological obligations each. All nine stored sessions contain a
  HYPOTHESIS followed by a hash-bound exact ZERO/CERTIFIED receipt.
- The `(2,2)` and `(2,1,1)` arity-four multiplicities make the evaluator
  program genuinely R3. Primitive construction succeeds without a named
  `HERMITE_DD` operator.

## Why admission fails

The article supplies the general higher-order Fréchet derivative theorem, not
the public member expressions. The three diagonal/rank-one direction tuples,
selected components, and expanded scalar formulas are benchmark-author
constructions. Their stated selection criterion is exactly that they isolate
the arity-four Hermite partitions the grammar is intended to test. No primary
source equation or independent scientific calculation motivates this
three-member catalog.

More decisively, historical TEST case `mp-opitz-dd-01` already specifies the
generic divided-difference table for arbitrary nodes, explicitly including
coincident nodes and non-polynomial functions with Hermite jets. Taking
`f=exp` and the Q7V3 node sequences produces the same hidden targets. Q7V3 is
therefore an instance of an inspected old TEST identity, which the new program
forbids mining as a fresh case. `mp-hermite-fA-01`, `test-a-hermite-two`,
`sciml-phi-hermite-01`, and current candidate C3J9 reinforce the proximity.

The package’s exact-byte duplicate scan is correct, but its manual review omits
the two strongest generic superfamilies: `mp-opitz-dd-01` and
`mp-hermite-fA-01`. A new arity/multiplicity partition is not sufficient to
turn a generic historical identity into fresh science.

## Allowed disposition

Keep the package as `DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY` for arity-four compilation, primitive
grammar expressibility, receipt replay, and search-frontier stress tests. It
must not fill a fresh scientific DEV slot or support a generalization claim.
