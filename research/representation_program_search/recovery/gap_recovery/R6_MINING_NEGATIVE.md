# R6 bounded mining result

Disposition: `NO_DEFENSIBLE_R6_CANDIDATE`.

No R6 package was created. The bounded scan required at least two operator
types with reuse across several nontrivial members, exact executable
reconstruction, a fresh scientific identity, and a proposer surface that did
not expose the target. It also rejected derivative/response graphs, short
recurrence ladders, scalar CSE, and sources that directly state the proposed
master.

The strongest fresh scientific object was the Debye-potential reconstruction
of time-dependent Maxwell fields. Greengard, Hagstrom, and Jiang give the
electric and magnetic fields in terms of curl, curl-of-curl, time derivative,
and vector-spherical-harmonic reconstruction in equations (3)–(7). That is a
real multi-operator object, but those operations have no semantics in the
frozen grammar/parser, and equation (3) directly supplies the representation.
Scalarizing fixed components would erase the vector master.

A fresh Potts-model transfer-matrix family likewise requires matrix powers,
spectral reconstruction, trace, and determinant; equation (5) directly gives
the transfer representation. These operations are outside frozen M1, and a
fixed-width scalar expansion would be a source-given sum rather than latent
induction.

Parser-feasible alternatives did not clear R6. DLMF Rayleigh formulas are a
short derivative ladder and overlap the existing `rps-real-c8q2` spherical
Bessel family. The Higham–Relton block-exponential identity is already mined,
is a derivative/response object, and needs matrix-block semantics. The frozen
van der Waals candidate was independently downgraded to R1.
