Owner: C1 (`OWNERS.md`). Domain `matrix`.

Fréchet / degenerate-eigenvalue identities. They are not admitted DEV/TEST
tasks. Analytic-domain predicates are copied from the cited theorem statement
and labeled `DECLARED` or `DERIVED` only. Physical folklore is not inserted.

Daleckii–Krein, Hermite f(A), Cauchy–Dunford, Kato simple-ev, Parlett, Van Loan,
Mathias, Opitz, Guo, or renamed symbols of those). Guo is sealed (`is_guo=false`).
