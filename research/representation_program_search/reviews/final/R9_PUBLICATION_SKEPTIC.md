## Decision

**F — STRUCTURED SEARCH ALSO FAILS TO SUPPORT REPRESENTATION INVENTION.**

This decision is defensible only with the following qualification attached to
it wherever the verdict appears:

> Under the frozen V1 rules, this bounded research run did not assemble the
> mandatory fresh scientific DEV calibration suite. The protocol therefore
> stopped before scientific search treatment. It produced no supporting
> evidence for representation invention, but it did not empirically test the
> efficacy of structured search, an AI heuristic, verifier feedback, or SOL.

The emphasized distinction is substantive, not editorial. “Fails to support”
means that this closed experiment contributes no positive evidence for the
hypothesis. It must not be paraphrased as “structured search failed.”

## Strongest defensible publication framing

The strongest defensible result is a **bounded negative feasibility result for
the joint experimental object**:

> A typed, exact-verification-aware representation-search stack was built and
> software-tested, but the project could not form its predeclared R2/R3/R4-or-
> R5/R6/negative scientific calibration suite without relaxing at least one
> frozen requirement on freshness, source grounding, assumptions, leakage,
> executable scientific IR, or case admission. The fail-closed protocol
> therefore withheld all scientific method comparisons.

This framing makes two legitimate contributions visible:

1. The implementation is auditable infrastructure: typed programs and states,
   deterministic serialization, bounded legal-action searches, constrained
   LLM roles, exact sessioned evaluation, grammar controls, matched controls,
   and fail-closed result handling exist and are exercised by software tests.
2. The admission process exposed a practical mismatch between the intended
   R3--R6 scientific frontier and what could be packaged freshly and
   executably under the frozen V1 language and benchmark rules.

Neither contribution establishes a representation-discovery method. The
closure report is therefore a research-line record and negative process
result, not a method paper, benchmark paper, or systems paper declared ready.

## Positive overclaim stress test

The following tempting claims do not survive review:

### “The program-search method works at R2”

`C9H4` has exact, independently replayed ZERO obligations under three grammar
encodings. Those receipts show that a human-authored R2 reference program can
be represented and certified. No S0--S7 or F0 search discovered it. Its public
formulas are already strongly factorized and the source discusses divided
differences. The allowed claim is constructor-and-verifier feasibility for one
R2 calibration identity, not search success.

### “The primitive grammar invents divided-difference structure”

The primitive reference encoding demonstrates expressibility. No grammar-
ablation search ran, so the experiment does not show compositional discovery,
operator-name de-leaking in practice, or grammar advantage.

### “The implementation is a systems/verification paper ready for release”

Extensive tests support internal software behavior, not scientific utility,
search-space coverage, external validity, scalability, or comparative system
performance. The central scientific evaluation was never reached. Decision D
would therefore promote engineering completeness into publication readiness
without the required evidence.

### “The benchmark effort itself is a paper-ready contribution”

The intended benchmark was not assembled or frozen; four mandatory DEV slots
were missing and there is no fresh TEST. The case audits and leakage controls
are valuable diagnostics, but they do not constitute the promised R2--R8
benchmark. Decision C is not supported.

### “The approach is promising but merely needs more evidence”

The infrastructure is substantial, but “promising” is an affirmative
scientific judgment. No eligible search observation supports it. Decision E
would blur implemented machinery with demonstrated method potential.

## Negative overclaim stress test

The closure also does not support stronger negative conclusions:

1. **No algorithmic failure was observed.** Enumerative, random, symbolic,
   SOL-guided, verifier-guided, LLM-guided, and free-form conditions have no
   eligible scientific outcomes in this experiment.
2. **No AI null was estimated.** There was no live scientific LLM decision,
   no matched search comparison, and no seed replication. The experiment says
   neither that AI helps nor that it is useless.
3. **No verifier or SOL effect was estimated.** Diagnostic verification and
   historical SOL artifacts are not search-treatment observations.
4. **No universal grammar inadequacy was proved.** The evidence concerns a
   bounded, hash-bound registry under joint V1 constraints. It does not prove
   that no admissible R3--R6 case exists or that the grammar alone caused the
   gate failure.
5. **`PACKAGING_GAP` is not mathematical impossibility.** Genuine matrix,
   block, noncommutative, integral, and tensor mathematics was found, but the
   frozen executable IR could not preserve its operational depth.
6. **Diagnostic exactness is not freshness or discovery.** Q7V3 is a valid R3
   compiler diagnostic with exact receipts, yet remains ineligible because of
   benchmark-author construction and historical-superfamily duplication.
7. **The previous Publication F was not replicated by a new efficacy test.**
   The old free-form negative result remains the historical motivation. This
   new line adds a pre-treatment feasibility closure, not a head-to-head
   comparison showing that structured search “also” performs poorly.

Accordingly, the phrase “the true R2/R3 frontier” must be handled as an
evidence frontier: this experiment adds an admitted R2 reference-program
control and no fresh R3 search evidence. It does not locate the intrinsic
capability frontier of structured search.

## Required wording and disposition

The final scientific report should lead with:

> **Publication F. The mandatory scientific DEV suite was not constructed in
> the bounded frozen experiment, so no search method or AI heuristic received
> a scientific trial. Structured representation-program search remains
> unsupported, not empirically refuted.**

Every result section for S0--S7, F0, grammar ablations, budget curves,
DeepSeek robustness, AI advantage, grammar advantage, verifier feedback, SOL,
and held-out generalization should report **NOT RUN / NOT ESTIMABLE**, never a
zero success rate. The absence of a TEST freeze manifest should be stated,
not repaired after closure. The repertoire should retain the implementation as
`IMPLEMENTED_UNEVALUATED` infrastructure and preserve all case packages,
diagnostics, proof gaps, and packaging gaps without upgrading them to supported
scientific search capabilities.

No paper package is warranted from this experiment before independent new
evidence. If the work is ever discussed publicly, the publishable unit is the
fail-closed feasibility audit and its implications for benchmark/IR design,
not a claim that formal search or AI heuristics were tested and found wanting.

## Final recommendation

Issue exactly **F — STRUCTURED SEARCH ALSO FAILS TO SUPPORT REPRESENTATION
INVENTION**, with the mandatory qualifier that this was a bounded
pre-treatment gate closure. This is the least misleading option in the fixed
taxonomy and the only one that neither advertises an unsupported positive
method result nor invents an empirical null.
