## 1. Authenticity and role of the admitted R2 case

`C9H4` is a genuine source-derived mathematical-physics case. The stored
excerpts identify the planar circular restricted three-body problem, its
Jacobi integral, the first-order divided-difference calculus, and the four
reciprocal-square-root coordinate identities. The repaired dossier also
corrects an earlier locator error: the retained identities are the unnumbered
lines immediately before the later `R3B_RHS` environment, not equation (28).

The executable representation is correctly assessed as R2: a single latent
kernel

```text
F(z) = 1/sqrt(z)
```

is evaluated on four pairs of distinct squared-distance nodes and followed by
exact linear reconstruction. No repeated node occurs. The 12 stored receipts
and 12 independent replays establish exact ZERO for the four obligations in
each grammar arm. The primitive arm uses only `VALUE` and
`LINEAR_COMBINATION`, so success does not depend on handing the method a named
`NEWTON_DD` primitive.

This is suitable for an R2 calibration slot, but not as discovery evidence.
The source itself develops divided differences and displays the reciprocal
square-root formula; the public formulas are already strongly factorized; and
the package is a repair of one previously rejected package, not a second mined
identity. The admission audit states all three limitations correctly.

## 2. Assumptions and domains

The R2 assumption contract is sufficient and does not import folklore. Real
coordinate and mass variables follow the source setting; `alpha+beta=1` is
source-declared without adding positivity; and the package explicitly limits
itself to the nondegenerate stratum in which the old/new radicands are positive
and unequal. Nonvanishing square roots, node differences, and displayed
denominators then follow on that stratum. This is a scoped calibration domain,
not a claim about every physical trajectory.

One reporting nuance must remain visible: relational predicates such as
positivity, inequality, and `alpha+beta=1` live in the scientific assumption
contract rather than the verifier's symbol flags. The ZERO receipts certify
the compiled symbolic equalities; they do not independently establish those
domain predicates. The existing dossier says this explicitly and should be
the wording used in final reports.

The R4/R5 recovery audit handles a more delicate domain issue correctly. For
the SPD coefficient, the source identity
`log(x/y)=log(x)-log(y)` is sound for positive spectral values, but the frozen
namespace exposes the verifier to a broader real domain. The retained NONZERO
diagnostic on that broader domain is therefore a refutation of the proposed
lowering **under the executable namespace**, not a refutation of the SPD
formula. Likewise, the polygamma recurrence's UNKNOWN is a proof gap, not a
missing human assumption and not an exact identity.

## 3. Honesty of the R3 boundary

The R3 exclusions are mathematically well-founded.

`Q7V3` is mechanically excellent: it compiles non-tautologically, represents
arity-four repeated-node partitions, survives primitive construction, and has
nine exact ZERO receipts. It nevertheless fails scientific admission because
the paper provides a general higher Fréchet-derivative theorem while the three
scalar members, direction tuples, and selected matrix components were created
by the benchmark author specifically to expose desired Hermite partitions.
More decisively, the hidden targets are instances of the already inspected
arbitrary-node historical TEST superfamily `mp-opitz-dd-01`. Exactness and R3
depth do not make the identity fresh.

The final bounded R3 audit also screened genuine scientific material:
MassToMI repeated mass-insertion divided differences, higher matrix-function
derivatives in a real-Hermitian spectral setting, and the flavour-expansion
theorem. These are scientifically real. They do not supply a fresh,
source-displayed, assumption-complete, target-blind catalog outside the old
Opitz/Hermite superfamilies. Rejecting them avoids the misleading move of
renaming a generic historical identity with new physical vocabulary.

The correct claim is `R3_MISSING` in this bounded, hash-bound mining pass. It
is not “scientific R3 identities do not exist,” and it is not “the search
could not find R3.” No search method was run.

## 4. R4/R5 scientific boundary

The two strongest R4/R5 leads are authentic but ineligible. The Hiai--Petz
positive-definite-matrix kernels are direct Newton/piecewise divided-difference
instances already present in inspected TEST. The Bouchard SPD coefficient has
a genuine coalesced stratum, but after the positive-domain logarithm rewrite
it becomes the historical Hermite-two template for `F(z)=z*log(z)`; without
positivity in the executable namespace the lowering is not sound. A
source-valid positive-domain identity therefore cannot be promoted merely
because a related algebraic form verifies elsewhere.

The audit also avoids inflating the R5 label. Merely parsing `polygamma`, or
evaluating a fixed special-function value, is not an operational
special-function representation change. The surviving fixed spherical-Bessel
chain `C8Q2` is correctly downgraded to R1 at best: one sinc seed, one
derivative, and one fixed linear recurrence do not construct a symbolic-order
special-function family. Its visible adjacent-order roles and post-package
reachability tests make it a diagnostic, not independent scientific evidence.

## 5. R6 and the scientific-IR packaging gap

The R6 audit identifies genuine multi-operator mathematics: block-matrix
Fréchet constructions, functional calculus, `dexp`/inverse-`dexp` and Magnus
relations, response operators, transfer matrices, vector harmonics, and tensor
projectors. Their scientific legitimacy does not make them executable under
the frozen Program IR. The present `MATRIX_FUNCTION` tag still parses a scalar
SymPy core; it has no matrix multiplication, block projection, spectral
calculus, noncommutative adjoint tower, trace/determinant, vector/tensor
contraction, or integral semantics.

The audit appropriately tests scalar lowerings rather than simply accepting
labels. Those lowerings collapse the apparent R6 cases to R0--R3, reveal the
master as a public member, duplicate a historical family, or fail strict M1
loading. Exact scalar receipts certify only those scalar reconstructions; they
cannot certify a block, tensor, or operator-level master that the IR never
represented.

Thus `R6_MISSING / PACKAGING_GAP` is correct. It is bounded to the audited
registry and frozen language. It is neither a theorem that R6 abstractions do
not exist nor evidence against any search algorithm. The final report should
prefer the phrase “scientific-IR packaging gap” over a generic “search
failure.”
