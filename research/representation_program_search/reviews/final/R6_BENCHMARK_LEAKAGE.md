## Gate finding

The `GATE_BLOCKED` decision is supported. Only the R2 slot is independently
admitted. R3, R4/R5, and R6 lack admissible cases, so the gate would remain
blocked even if the negative-control interface were repaired. The prohibition
on scientific DEV execution, TEST freeze, held-out evaluation, and method
interpretation is therefore necessary. In particular, an exact reference
program or a collection of ZERO receipts is not a search result.

The closure's causal boundary is also correct: there is no basis for
`PROGRAM_SUCCESS`, `AI_SEARCH_ADVANTAGE`, `GRAMMAR_ADVANTAGE`, search-budget
curves, or conclusions about AI ranking, AI action proposal, symbolic
heuristics, SOL, verifier feedback, or free-form F0. Those quantities are not
zero; they are **not estimable because the comparison was not run**.

## Operator-name and target-leakage controls

The formal controls are well chosen but unevaluated scientifically. Public
proposer views forbid target types, gold programs, operator sequences, and
hidden roles. `G_NO_HERMITE` and `G_PRIMITIVE` address the risk that a named
`HERMITE_DD` primitive gives away the intended representation. C9H4 and Q7V3
show that their evaluator reference programs are expressible without the named
primitive.

That last fact is only an expressibility/compilation control. It does not show
that frozen search can discover the primitive construction, nor that operator
leakage would not affect search. No grammar-ablation search ran. The final
report must therefore describe these controls as implemented and mechanically
validated, not as causal ablation results.

Leakage also occurs during benchmark construction, not only on the proposer
surface. Q7V3 is the clearest example: its public projection is opaque, yet the
benchmark author selected scalarizations specifically for grammar-relevant
multiplicities. Likewise, source documents or assumptions that explicitly say
“recurrence,” “divided difference,” or expose adjacent-order roles can supply
the representation before search even if the gold program is hidden. The
manual case-skeptic decisions correctly treat this as a scientific-admission
issue.

## Negative-trap eligibility

The six M10 traps are correctly excluded from the mandatory search gate under
their committed contract: they are synthetic, evaluator-only fixtures; their
hidden programs and expected outcomes are implementation oracles, and their
README forbids entry into DEV, TEST, or CHALLENGE. The separate ZERO positive
control is not a negative case. None can be relabeled after inspection to fill
the missing calibration slot.

There is, however, an internal calibration-interface gap that should be stated
explicitly. The experiment required one runnable negative trap and also built
M10 traps, but the implemented trap format was deliberately made unusable as a
public search-method input. Thus `NEGATIVE_TRAP: MISSING` is correct under the
frozen artifacts, while the reason is a design/packaging mismatch—not evidence
that a suitable negative scientific case cannot exist. This mismatch does not
change the gate outcome because three positive depth slots are independently
missing.

## Freshness audit limits and overclaim controls

The no-TEST-freeze and no-advantage boundaries are enforced correctly. The
following wording constraints are needed in the final synthesis:

1. Say that the suite **could not be constructed in the bounded experiment**.
   The audits do not prove global infeasibility under the frozen grammar/IR for
   every scientific source that might later be found.
2. The 117-file R3 record is a complete point-in-time path/hash inventory, not
   an exhaustive semantic comparison of all pairs. Only the identified
   decisive controls received manual semantic adjudication. It supports the
   bounded `R3_MISSING` result, not a theorem that no fresh R3 case exists.
3. Do not turn diagnostic/reference-program ZERO receipts into discovery,
   method, grammar, or AI success. This applies especially to Q7V3 and C3J9.
4. Do not report missing method results as failures, zero success rates, or
   unfavorable curves. “Not run / not estimable” is the only supported result.
5. Do not call C9H4 a fresh held-out case, count its predecessor separately,
   or use it to soften the R3+ frontier failure.
6. Do not claim the primitive-grammar ablation controlled leakage in practice;
   only reference-program expressibility was established.
7. `PACKAGING_GAP` remains separate from mathematical nonexistence and from
   method failure.

## Bottom line

The strongest benchmark-integrity result is negative but clean: the project
refused to manufacture the missing DEV suite from old TEST superfamilies,
benchmark-authored scalarizations, shallow special-function labels,
evaluator-only traps, or packaging gaps. That protects the causal experiment
from leakage, but it also leaves the central program-search hypothesis
untested. Publication closure should preserve precisely that distinction.
