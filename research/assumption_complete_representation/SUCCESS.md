# Success definitions

## SCIENTIFIC_DD_OK

Real scientific source; grounded members; explicit F; explicit DD
nodes; reconstruction; analytic assumptions sufficient; compiled
obligations; ZERO. Track Newton / Hermite / special-function DD
separately.

## SCIENTIFIC_MASTER_OK

≥2 structurally distinct grounded members; explicit F; ≥1 nontrivial
operator; all claimed instances ZERO; assumptions explicit;
non-tautological. Stronger: ≥3 members or ≥2 operator classes.

## AI_UNIQUE_SUCCESS

Strongest frozen symbolic stack fails to produce the operational
representation; LLM produces it; grounding; complete assumptions;
compile; ZERO; no gold leakage; non-tautological.

## PROBLEM_UNDERSPECIFIED

Verifier cannot adjudicate because a required analytic-domain
predicate is NOT_DECLARED. Not a method failure.

neg ZERO + C0 ZERO + remainder UNKNOWN ⇒ UNKNOWN, never ZERO.
