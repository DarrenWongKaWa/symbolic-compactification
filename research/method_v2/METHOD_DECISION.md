## Dominant bottleneck

**A — proposer architecture** (one isolated scientific proposer, not an ensemble).

A small, inseparable piece of **B** is included: the existing
`SCIENTIFIC_STRUCTURE_PROPOSER` objective. It is not a second project.
Verifier coverage (C), a new IR (D), and metric replacement (E) are
**rejected as the v2 intervention**.

## Observed failure

Certified workflows stop at D1/D2 (linearity, common factor). Higher-level
hypotheses arrive as unexpanded names, prose, or unsafe slogans. The
verifier then returns UNKNOWN (unexpanded / unsupported syntax) or
correctly refuses drop-Piecewise and domain-illegal recurrences.

## Evidence

- D2 kernel merge: ZERO for R1–R5 **and** named transforms. Search is
  not the D2 bottleneck.
- D3 names (`Phi`, `PsiThermal`) verify ZERO **only when expanded**;
  unexpanded Function names are UNKNOWN.
- D4 drop generic `K(x,y)`: UNKNOWN, blank claimed proven.
- Fermi `-1/z`: not ZERO. Must not become an engine identity.
- Guo skill 2026: stop after L2 ZERO.

## Causal hypothesis

If the searcher (i) is isolated, (ii) **must expand definitions to a
closed expression before verify**, (iii) **does not halt at the first
D1/D2 ZERO**, and (iv) consumes residual/UNKNOWN feedback, then
certified D-level and closed-candidate rate rise **without** increasing
false promotions.

## One proposed intervention

**Method v2 orchestrator** (research layer, engine ZERO/NONZERO/UNKNOWN
unchanged):

```
current
  → isolated scientific proposer (one)
  → expand hypothesis_definitions → closed candidate
  → verify_equivalent
  → ZERO: record certified current, **continue** until step budget
  → NONZERO/UNKNOWN: feed residual/kind back; do not promote
```

Named production transforms may run as the first cheap step. They must
not terminate the loop.

## Success criterion (DEV only)

On `research/search_bottleneck/dev_hard/` plus any new DEV items:

1. False promotions = 0.
2. Closed-candidate rate (parseable expanded text) **strictly higher**
   than M0 on items where M0 emitted unexpanded names or prose.
3. D2 ZERO rate **not lower** than M0.
4. At least **two** items show a higher *certified* scientific level
   (named generator/master that **expands to ZERO**, or extra certified
   step after first D1/D2) versus M0 stop-at-first-ZERO.

## Falsification

M1 is **abandoned** if (1) false promotion > 0, or (2) D2 ZERO regresses,
or (3) closed-candidate rate does not rise and no item gains certified
level. One revision of expansion/loop is allowed; no third rewrite.
