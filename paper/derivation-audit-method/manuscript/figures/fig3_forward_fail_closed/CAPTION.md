# Figure 3

**Title.** Fail-closed promotion of a working expression.

**Caption.**

Forward derivation promotes a candidate only on admissible evidence.
From a current state \(E_t\), a valid equivalent (A) returns engine
`ZERO` and may become \(E_{t+1}\); a sign or factor error (B) returns
`NONZERO` and is refused; an undecidable or unsupported candidate (C)
returns `UNKNOWN` and is refused. In both refusals the scientific state
remains \(E_t\), so a later valid candidate can still be accepted. The
panel reports the frozen forward-replay control: false promotion \(0/36\)
on injected invalids, under the verdict Forward workflow demonstrated
with caveats. It is a gating diagram, not a proposer ranking, and it
does not claim autonomous discovery.
