# Figure 1

**Title.** One typed evidence graph, two operating modes.

**Caption.**

A single typed evidence graph supports two scientific jobs. Forward
constructs the next working state: an untrusted candidate \(H_i\) (human,
CAS, or optional model) is compiled to a claim and judged before it may
replace \(E_t\). Audit inspects a printed manuscript: extracted relations
are likewise untrusted until judged. Both directions share the same
object \((E_i,E_j,\tau,c,\text{source},\text{state})\), where \(\tau\) is
the claimed mathematical move and \(c\) is the certificate class.
Admissible evidence may promote a state or fill a verified table;
`NONZERO`, `UNKNOWN`, and unsupported claims are refused. Independent
checking of untrusted producers is prior art; the figure's object is a
manuscript-native physics derivation edge, not the existence of a checker.
