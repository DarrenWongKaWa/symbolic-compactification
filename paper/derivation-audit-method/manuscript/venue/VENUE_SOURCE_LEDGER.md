## A. Computer Physics Communications (Elsevier)

| Item | Official source | Retrieved claim |
|---|---|---|
| Aims / focus | Elsevier journal shop / CPC description, ISSN 0010-4655 | Publishes research papers and application software in computational physics. Focus: contemporary computational methods and techniques and their implementation, the effectiveness of which will normally be evidenced in a **substantive problem in physics**. |
| Article types | Same + Guide for authors | Two types: **Computational Physics Papers (CP)** and **Computer Programs in Physics (CPiP)**. |
| CP themes | Same | Mathematical and numerical methods and algorithms; computational models; **algebraic computation**. Each will normally include software implementation and performance details. Software ideally available via GitHub, Zenodo, or an institutional repository. |
| CPiP | Same | Full papers describing significant programs archived in the CPC Program Library (Mendeley Data). Approved open-source licence required. |
| Editorial-desk bar | Elsevier shop CPC description | Introduction directed to a general audience. Authors must clearly articulate novelty and significance and how the paper will advance the solution of an important physics application. Papers that fail this, in a Principal Editor’s opinion, will not be sent for review. |
| Guide for authors | https://www.elsevier.com/journals/computer-physics-communications/0010-4655/guide-for-authors (redirects to ScienceDirect publish/guide-for-authors) | Numbered sections; software should be cited as software; dataset identifiers. |
| Open access | Elsevier journal model for CPC | Hybrid: subscription plus optional gold OA (standard Elsevier CPC model as of access date). Not diamond OA. |
| Generative AI | https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/generative-ai-policies-for-journals (policy updated June 2026) | Authors may use AI with human oversight. Disclose manuscript-preparation AI in a declaration before references. Research-process AI belongs in Methods. AI cannot be an author. Grammar/spelling checkers need no declaration. Reviewers must not upload manuscripts to AI tools. |
| Length | Guide for authors | No CPC-specific short-article length cap comparable to an APS Letter. CP is a full research paper. |

Official URLs recorded:

- https://shop.elsevier.com/journals/computer-physics-communications/0010-4655
- https://www.elsevier.com/journals/computer-physics-communications/0010-4655/guide-for-authors
- https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/generative-ai-policies-for-journals
