# Frozen paper claims (v0.3.0-alpha authority)

Central question:

> How can constructive symbolic derivation and retrospective manuscript
> audit share one machine-auditable evidence contract, without granting a
> proposer (human, rule, or AI) the authority to certify its own claims?

Software authority: tag `v0.3.0-alpha` (see `SOFTWARE_AUTHORITY.md`).

## The paper MAY claim

- verified iterative symbolic reasoning (Forward)
- machine-auditable manuscript derivations (Audit)
- one typed evidence graph for both workflows
- fail-closed candidate adjudication
- verification-gated state advancement
- generated reviewer evidence
- complete numbered-equation inventory of one public theoretical paper
- source-grounded relation checking on that inventory
- a sampled multi-paper stress test (not five complete-paper proofs)
- explicit distinction between local exact and theorem-mediated certificates
- `ZERO ≠ CERTIFIED_BY_RULE`

## The paper MUST NOT claim

- robust autonomous representation invention
- reliable context-conditioned scientific discovery
- general theorem proving
- full automatic paper proof
- that 189 Guo equations were all certified
- verification of physical conclusions
- universal simplification
- complete exact limit/remainder certification
- automatic understanding of arbitrary papers/PDFs
- that a language model reliably discovers the correct next mathematical
  representation
- that all conceivable forgery is impossible
- that experimental approximation overlays are part of v0.3.0-alpha

## Forward (strongest supported claim)

A verified iterative symbolic-reasoning workflow in which candidate
transformations may be supplied by a human, rules, or an AI proposer, but
advancement requires independent fail-closed evidence.

Public product demos on `v0.3.0-alpha`: `examples/forward/exact-step`
(`ZERO`) and `examples/forward/refused-step` (`NONZERO`). Additional
evidence: session tests with a scripted proposer; masked forward replay on
`archive/forward-proposer-replay-v1` (false promotion 0/36 on injected
invalids; substitution-conditioned gold recovered as expressions but not
promoted versus current). Model-assisted proposal is experimental and is
not a shipped `propose` command. Context-conditioned representation
invention remains unestablished. TargetRecovery@K is not a paper headline.

## Audit (mature application)

Equation inventory of printed numbers, source-grounded typed relations,
parallel verification, rule certificates, structural records, generated
`RESULTS.md`.

## Approved public depth statement (Guo flagship)

> The same evidence framework has been exercised on a complete numbered-
> equation inventory of a published theoretical-physics derivation. This is
> an equation-level audit. It does not prove the paper or confirm its
> physical conclusions. Inventory coverage and certified residuals are
> different numbers. The case is formative field validation, not held-out
> generalisation.

Approved public metrics (Guo et al., arXiv:2511.16422v2), from
`examples/flagship/guo/RESULTS.md` on `v0.3.0-alpha`:

```text
numbered equations inventoried = 189/189
derivation relations in the public table = 146
executable numbered relations = 53  (+ 1 Leibniz helper)
EXACT_ZERO = 32
ZERO_UNDER_SUBSTITUTION = 21
CERTIFIED_BY_RULE = 11
UNKNOWN_REMAINDER = 17
STRUCTURAL = 47
UNSUPPORTED = 18
NONZERO = 0
false promotion on injected controls = 0/155
```

The earlier selected-edge table (`archive/guo-selected-edge-validation-v1`)
is a precursor, not the flagship public result.

## Approved public breadth statement

> A sampled stress test on five public theory papers (41 source-grounded
> edges) preserves the same status vocabulary. False promotion on injected
> invalids was 0/30. This is not five complete-paper audits.

## RQ4 candidate (not a claim of this draft)

Approximation-mediated reasoning: provenance of an approximation is
independent of exact verification of algebra after that approximation.
`ZERO` must not be weakened. Experiment
`archive/approximation-authority-v1` distinguished four diagnostic cases.
Those overlays were not productized in `v0.3.0-alpha`.

## Private-manuscript policy

Unpublished local scientific manuscripts are excluded.
